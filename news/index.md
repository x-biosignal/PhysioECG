# Changelog

## PhysioECG 0.4.1

### New features

- [`ecgHRVautonomic()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVautonomic.md)
  — cardiac autonomic indices (Toichi 1997) from the Poincare 4-SD box:
  the Cardiac Sympathetic Index (CSI = L/T = SD2/SD1), the Cardiac Vagal
  Index (CVI = log10(L\*T)), and the Modified CSI (L^2/T; Jeppesen 2014,
  seizure detection). SD1 = SDSD/sqrt(2) (universal), SD2 = geometric
  paired-projection. Reproduces NeuroKit2’s `hrv_nonlinear`
  CSI/CVI/CSI_Modified bit-for-bit.

## PhysioECG 0.4.0

### New features

- [`ecgHRVasymmetry()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVasymmetry.md)
  — heart-rate asymmetry (HRA) Poincare-plot asymmetry descriptors
  (Guzik 2006; Piskorski & Guzik 2011): the Guzik/Slope/Area/Porta
  indices (GI/SI/AI/PI) and the deceleration/acceleration contributions
  to short-term (C1d/C1a), long-term (C2d/C2a), and total (Cd/Ca) HRV.
  Quantifies the temporal irreversibility of heart-rate dynamics —
  decelerations (RR prolongations) and accelerations (shortenings)
  contribute unequally to variability. Complements the symmetric
  [`ecgHRVpoincare()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVpoincare.md)
  (SD1/SD2). Reproduces NeuroKit2’s `hrv_nonlinear` HRA indices
  bit-for-bit (max \|diff\| ~1e-14).

## PhysioECG 0.3.2

### Bug fixes

- [`ecgDFA()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDFA.md)
  (and the internal `.dfa_alpha1()` used by
  [`ecgDFArolling()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDFArolling.md))
  now generate **logarithmically-spaced** scales instead of
  linearly-spaced ones. With linear spacing, a long RR series (where
  `n/4` is far larger than 64) left the short-range window `[4, 16]`
  with a single scale and the long-range window `[16, 64]` empty, so the
  slope fits returned `NA` —
  [`ecgDFA()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDFA.md)
  produced `alpha1 = alpha2 = NA` on real overnight recordings
  (thousands of beats) even though it worked on short (a
  few-hundred-beat) test series. DFA requires log-spaced scales; both
  windows are now populated and `alpha1`/`alpha2` are computed
  correctly. Verified on synthetic signals with known exponents (white
  noise → α ≈ 0.5, random walk → α ≈ 1.5) and against `nolds.dfa`. Added
  regression tests covering a long (6000-beat) series and the known
  exponents.

## PhysioECG 0.3.1

### Validation

- Added a real MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database validation test
  (`test-mitbih-validation.R`, fixture
  `tests/testthat/fixtures/mitbih-reference.rds`, generated by
  `data-raw/ecg_mitbih_reference.{py,R}`), replacing the synthetic-RR
  substitute in the ECG “real data” path (VAL-05).
  [`ecgDetectRpeaks()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDetectRpeaks.md)
  is scored against cardiologist beat annotations (AAMI EC57, +/-150
  ms): F1 = 1.0 on the clean record 100 and F1 = 0.97 on the noisiest
  record 203.
  [`ecgHRVtime()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVtime.md)
  reproduces the standard SDNN/RMSSD/pNN50 definitions exactly on a
  5-minute real RR series and agrees with RHRV to \< 1%.

## PhysioECG 0.2.0

Initial release of PhysioECG as a standalone package in the x-biosignal
ecosystem, split out from the PhysioExperiment monolith. Provides
electrocardiography (ECG) analysis for `PhysioExperiment` objects,
spanning R-peak detection, heart rate variability (HRV), waveform
morphology, and signal-quality assessment.

### New Features

- Multi-detector QRS / R-peak detection via
  [`ecgDetectRpeaks()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDetectRpeaks.md),
  offering four algorithms behind a common bandpass front end with
  automatic inverted-lead handling: adaptive Pan-Tompkins (default),
  Hamilton, Elgendi, and Christov.
  [`ecgRRintervals()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgRRintervals.md)
  derives the RR-interval series that feeds every HRV routine.

- Time-domain HRV via
  [`ecgHRVtime()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVtime.md)
  (SDNN, RMSSD, pNN50, mean RR, mean HR), with geometric indices
  ([`ecgHRVgeometric()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVgeometric.md);
  triangular index and TINN) and long-term segmented indices
  ([`ecgHRVsegmented()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVsegmented.md);
  SDANN, SDNN index).

- Frequency-domain HRV via
  [`ecgHRVfreq()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVfreq.md),
  estimating VLF/LF/HF band power, LF/HF ratio, normalized units, and
  in-band peak frequencies from a Welch, Lomb-Scargle, or Burg
  autoregressive spectrum, with optional smoothness-priors detrending of
  the resampled tachogram.

- Nonlinear HRV:

  - [`ecgHRVpoincare()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVpoincare.md)
    for Poincare descriptors (SD1, SD2, SD1/SD2).
  - [`ecgSampleEntropy()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgSampleEntropy.md)
    for regularity of the RR series.
  - [`ecgDFA()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDFA.md)
    for detrended fluctuation analysis, plus
    [`ecgHRVnonlinear()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgHRVnonlinear.md)
    as a convenience wrapper combining the nonlinear metrics.
  - [`ecgDFArolling()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDFArolling.md)
    for a time-resolved DFA alpha1 trajectory and
    [`dfaThresholdCrossings()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/dfaThresholdCrossings.md)
    for the Gronwald & Rogers exercise-intensity thresholds (AT1 alpha1
    = 0.75, AT2 alpha1 = 0.5).

- ECG waveform morphology via
  [`ecgDelineate()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDelineate.md)
  (QRS onset/offset, P- and T-wave peaks per beat) and
  [`ecgIntervals()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgIntervals.md),
  which reports PR, QT, and QRS durations with rate-corrected QT by
  Bazett, Fridericia, Framingham, and Hodges formulas.

- Rhythm gating via
  [`ecgDetectAF()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgDetectAF.md),
  classifying each channel as sinus, atrial fibrillation, or frequent
  ectopy from RR irregularity (ectopic fraction, RMSSD/mean-RR ratio,
  and normalized dRR Shannon entropy). The time-, frequency-, and
  nonlinear-domain HRV functions honor a `rhythm_check` argument that
  invalidates HRV metrics for non-sinus channels, where HRV is
  physiologically undefined.

- Signal quality and cleaning:

  - [`ecgSignalQuality()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgSignalQuality.md)
    for per-channel SNR, baseline wander, saturation, and a composite
    quality score.
  - [`ecgBeatSQI()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgBeatSQI.md)
    and
    [`ecgBeatSQIgate()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgBeatSQIgate.md)
    for a beat-level quality index from the agreement between two
    independent QRS detectors.
  - [`ecgQualityCheck()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgQualityCheck.md),
    [`ecgRRcorrect()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgRRcorrect.md),
    and
    [`ecgEctopicBurden()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgEctopicBurden.md)
    for ectopic-beat detection, linear or cubic-spline correction, and
    burden reporting.
  - [`ecgBaselineCorrect()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/ecgBaselineCorrect.md)
    for baseline-wander removal and
    [`smoothnessPriorsDetrend()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/smoothnessPriorsDetrend.md)
    for detrending a uniformly sampled series.

- Synthetic ECG generators for examples and testing:
  [`make_ecg()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/make_ecg.md),
  [`make_ecg_irregular()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/make_ecg_irregular.md),
  [`make_ecg_pqrst()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/make_ecg_pqrst.md),
  and
  [`make_ecg_noisy()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioECG/reference/make_ecg_noisy.md).
