# PhysioECG 0.4.2

## Minor improvements

* `ecgHRVgeometric()` now also accepts an RR **data frame** (with an `rr_ms` column, as produced by the
  RR-interval readers), not only a numeric vector — aligning its interface with `ecgHRVtime()`. Its HRV
  triangular index (HTI) is cross-tool certified against `NeuroKit2.hrv_time` HRV_HTI (bit-for-bit) on
  real Fantasia RR; TINN differs by a documented triangular-interpolation convention.

# PhysioECG 0.4.1

## New features

* `ecgHRVautonomic()` — cardiac autonomic indices (Toichi 1997) from the Poincare 4-SD box:
  the Cardiac Sympathetic Index (CSI = L/T = SD2/SD1), the Cardiac Vagal Index
  (CVI = log10(L*T)), and the Modified CSI (L^2/T; Jeppesen 2014, seizure detection). SD1 =
  SDSD/sqrt(2) (universal), SD2 = geometric paired-projection. Reproduces NeuroKit2's
  `hrv_nonlinear` CSI/CVI/CSI_Modified bit-for-bit.

# PhysioECG 0.4.0

## New features

* `ecgHRVasymmetry()` — heart-rate asymmetry (HRA) Poincare-plot asymmetry descriptors
  (Guzik 2006; Piskorski & Guzik 2011): the Guzik/Slope/Area/Porta indices (GI/SI/AI/PI) and
  the deceleration/acceleration contributions to short-term (C1d/C1a), long-term (C2d/C2a), and
  total (Cd/Ca) HRV. Quantifies the temporal irreversibility of heart-rate dynamics —
  decelerations (RR prolongations) and accelerations (shortenings) contribute unequally to
  variability. Complements the symmetric `ecgHRVpoincare()` (SD1/SD2). Reproduces NeuroKit2's
  `hrv_nonlinear` HRA indices bit-for-bit (max |diff| ~1e-14).

# PhysioECG 0.3.2

## Bug fixes

* `ecgDFA()` (and the internal `.dfa_alpha1()` used by `ecgDFArolling()`) now
  generate **logarithmically-spaced** scales instead of linearly-spaced ones.
  With linear spacing, a long RR series (where `n/4` is far larger than 64) left
  the short-range window `[4, 16]` with a single scale and the long-range window
  `[16, 64]` empty, so the slope fits returned `NA` — `ecgDFA()` produced
  `alpha1 = alpha2 = NA` on real overnight recordings (thousands of beats) even
  though it worked on short (a few-hundred-beat) test series. DFA requires
  log-spaced scales; both windows are now populated and `alpha1`/`alpha2` are
  computed correctly. Verified on synthetic signals with known exponents (white
  noise → α ≈ 0.5, random walk → α ≈ 1.5) and against `nolds.dfa`. Added
  regression tests covering a long (6000-beat) series and the known exponents.

# PhysioECG 0.2.0

Initial release of PhysioECG as a standalone package in the x-biosignal
ecosystem, split out from the PhysioExperiment monolith. Provides
electrocardiography (ECG) analysis for `PhysioExperiment` objects, spanning
R-peak detection, heart rate variability (HRV), waveform morphology, and
signal-quality assessment.

## New Features

- Multi-detector QRS / R-peak detection via `ecgDetectRpeaks()`, offering four
  algorithms behind a common bandpass front end with automatic inverted-lead
  handling: adaptive Pan-Tompkins (default), Hamilton, Elgendi, and Christov.
  `ecgRRintervals()` derives the RR-interval series that feeds every HRV
  routine.

- Time-domain HRV via `ecgHRVtime()` (SDNN, RMSSD, pNN50, mean RR, mean HR),
  with geometric indices (`ecgHRVgeometric()`; triangular index and TINN) and
  long-term segmented indices (`ecgHRVsegmented()`; SDANN, SDNN index).

- Frequency-domain HRV via `ecgHRVfreq()`, estimating VLF/LF/HF band power,
  LF/HF ratio, normalized units, and in-band peak frequencies from a
  Welch, Lomb-Scargle, or Burg autoregressive spectrum, with optional
  smoothness-priors detrending of the resampled tachogram.

- Nonlinear HRV:
  - `ecgHRVpoincare()` for Poincare descriptors (SD1, SD2, SD1/SD2).
  - `ecgSampleEntropy()` for regularity of the RR series.
  - `ecgDFA()` for detrended fluctuation analysis, plus `ecgHRVnonlinear()`
    as a convenience wrapper combining the nonlinear metrics.
  - `ecgDFArolling()` for a time-resolved DFA alpha1 trajectory and
    `dfaThresholdCrossings()` for the Gronwald & Rogers exercise-intensity
    thresholds (AT1 alpha1 = 0.75, AT2 alpha1 = 0.5).

- ECG waveform morphology via `ecgDelineate()` (QRS onset/offset, P- and
  T-wave peaks per beat) and `ecgIntervals()`, which reports PR, QT, and QRS
  durations with rate-corrected QT by Bazett, Fridericia, Framingham, and
  Hodges formulas.

- Rhythm gating via `ecgDetectAF()`, classifying each channel as sinus,
  atrial fibrillation, or frequent ectopy from RR irregularity (ectopic
  fraction, RMSSD/mean-RR ratio, and normalized dRR Shannon entropy). The
  time-, frequency-, and nonlinear-domain HRV functions honor a
  `rhythm_check` argument that invalidates HRV metrics for non-sinus channels,
  where HRV is physiologically undefined.

- Signal quality and cleaning:
  - `ecgSignalQuality()` for per-channel SNR, baseline wander, saturation,
    and a composite quality score.
  - `ecgBeatSQI()` and `ecgBeatSQIgate()` for a beat-level quality index from
    the agreement between two independent QRS detectors.
  - `ecgQualityCheck()`, `ecgRRcorrect()`, and `ecgEctopicBurden()` for
    ectopic-beat detection, linear or cubic-spline correction, and burden
    reporting.
  - `ecgBaselineCorrect()` for baseline-wander removal and
    `smoothnessPriorsDetrend()` for detrending a uniformly sampled series.

- Synthetic ECG generators for examples and testing: `make_ecg()`,
  `make_ecg_irregular()`, `make_ecg_pqrst()`, and `make_ecg_noisy()`.
